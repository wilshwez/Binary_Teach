Teacher's Tip

How to solve this

To convert the target number to binary quickly:

  1. Find the largest power of 2 that is **smaller than** the target.
  2. Toggle that bit **ON**.
  3. Subtract that power of 2 from your target number to find your new target remainder.
  4. Repeat the process with the remainder until your current sum matches the target!
